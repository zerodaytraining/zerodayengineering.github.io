Alpha Intelligence

Zero Day Engineering

As AI accelerates both exploit development and defensive automation, the vulnerability landscape becomes harder to navigate.

Alpha Intelligence offers the systems to cut through the noise to actionable signal, enabled by expert judgement of a frontier exploit engineer.

This isn't threat monitoring. Threat monitoring is overcomplicated dashboards, AI-generated summaries, generic copy-pasted information and anonymous analysts. Alpha Intelligence is curated insights, actionable briefings, and access to the offensive thinking of a named vulnerability researcher – a seat at the frontier of offensive exploit research.

Coverage: core stacks with consistent exploit exposure – browsers, hypervisors, kernels, office software; emerging stacks – AI infrastructure, crypto protocols and smart contracts; our picks.

Target audience: C-level and project decision makers, founders of security-critical startups, investors, HNWI.

Deep Alerts

Realtime notifications of actively exploited and likely exploitable vulnerabilities in critical software, with deep technical insights not present in public sources. Curated for real impact, corrected for hype and AI noise by the exploit specialist.

Frontier Expertise

We send a weekly low-pressure podcast covering the past week's signals, and bi-weekly analytical deep-dives in text, such as 0-day insights; with a strategic interpretation from the senior offensive expert.

Live Briefing

A monthly group call with the principal to review the month's landscape, highlight what matters, walk through the exploit code, and answer individual questions in a dedicated Q&A session.

Ask an Exploit Engineer

Ongoing scoped access to advanced offensive mindset for your defensive challenges – group-based and in a private channel.

Watch the Landscape

Experimental access to the founder's own monitoring platform with real-time feeds and curated selections.
