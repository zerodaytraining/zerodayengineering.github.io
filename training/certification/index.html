Certification

Zero Day Engineering

Two certificates are offered: Competence and Completion. Both are included with the Complete package and live courses, issued on explicit request. Hashes of issued certificates are published on the certified students page.

Certificate of Competence

Awarded on practical proof of competence: an independent project completed in limited time, evaluated for honest merit consistent with the course level. Closer to graduate-school discipline than undergraduate examination.

Certificate of Completion

Issued on request, on the student's word. No tests, no verification of practical work.

Classic Certificates

Completion only. Discontinued effective 1 August 2026. Highly valuable as a CV credential. Recipients took our trainings at an early stage — holding one signals critical thinking and discernment above average.
