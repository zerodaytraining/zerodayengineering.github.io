About

Zero Day Engineering offers a comprehensive system of training in vulnerability research and exploit development, from zero to hardcore. The Foundations programs comprise the system's core identity invariant - knowledge and skills applicable to any system - and essential to any workflow in our domain. Building on the Foundations, Specialized courses develop cutting edge class-specific and implementation-specific mastery. As an spin-off from our internal R&D work, this training system represents state-of-the-art in the offensive security field, expressed through a unique and proprietary methodology of a singular creator.
