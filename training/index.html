About

Zero Day Engineering offers a comprehensive system of training in vulnerability research and exploit development, from zero to hardcore.

The Foundations programs comprise the system's core that is applicable to any research target and essential to any workflow.

Building on the Foundations, Specialized courses develop cutting-edge class-specific and implementation-specific mastery.

As a distillation of our internal R&D work, this training system represents state-of-the-art in the offensive security field, expressed through a unique methodology of a singular creator.

The kernel of our methodology is Abstract Modeling: we show how to think in systems and use frameworks to generate leverage, as opposed to memorizing patterns and drilling skills. This discipline is a prerequisite to using AI as a force multiplier rather than as a workflow replacer.
